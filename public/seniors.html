Why Haus of Lyra

Not a volume studio. Not a template session.

Most senior photographers run 5 sessions a weekend. They've got a spot, a pose, and a preset. You show up, you smile, you leave. The photos look fine. You forget about them in a week.

That's not what we do. We take the time to get to know you — what makes you feel like yourself, what you're actually into, where you actually want to be. The session gets built around that.

We're selective because that's the only way to deliver something that actually means something. If you want something people are going to ask about — this is the place.

The Real Experience

We ease into it. No pressure the second you walk in.

A lot of seniors are nervous. That's completely normal — most people aren't used to being in front of a camera.

So we don't start that way. We grab coffee. We talk. We let the session build naturally until you stop thinking about the camera and start actually being yourself. That's when the real photos happen.

I use prompts and coaching instead of rigid posing. You'll look like you're actually living — not like you're trying to pose for a photo.
